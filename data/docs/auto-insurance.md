# Northwind Auto Insurance Policy Terms

## 4.1 Collision damage
Damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision is covered up to the vehicle's agreed value.
A deductible of NT$5,000 applies to each claim. Damage from wear and tear or mechanical failure is not covered.

## 4.2 Theft of the vehicle
If the insured vehicle is stolen, Northwind pays 80% of the agreed value.
The theft must be reported to the police within 24 hours of discovery, and a police report number is required.
If the vehicle is not recovered within 30 days of the report, the claim is paid. Theft of personal items left inside the vehicle is not covered.

## 4.3 Auto exclusions
No benefit is paid when the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs, was driving without a valid licence,
or was using the vehicle for racing or for paid ride-hailing services.

## 4.4 Rental car rider
Only for policies with the "rental-car" rider.
While the insured vehicle is being repaired after a covered claim, rental car costs are reimbursed up to NT$1,500 per day, for at most 10 days.

## 4.5 Windscreen damage
Windscreen and window glass damage is covered with no deductible, once per policy year.
