# Northwind Policy Changes, Cancellation and Refunds

## 7.1 Free-look period
A policyholder may cancel within 10 days of the policy start date and receive a full refund of the premium.

## 7.2 Cancellation after the free-look period
After the free-look period, the refund is the unused premium calculated pro rata,
minus an administration fee of 10% of the refund.

## 7.3 Travel policies
A travel insurance policy cannot be cancelled once the trip has started. No refund is given after the start date.

## 7.4 Changing address or beneficiary
Address changes can be made in the Northwind app at any time.
Changing the beneficiary requires a signed form from the policyholder and takes effect on the date the form is received.

## 7.5 Reinstating a lapsed policy
A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 6 months of lapsing by paying the overdue premium.
For health insurance, the waiting period starts again from the reinstatement date.
After 6 months, a new policy must be purchased.
