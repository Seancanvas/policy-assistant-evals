# Northwind Claims FAQ for Staff

## 8.1 Can a family member file a claim for the policyholder?
Yes, if they provide a signed power of attorney from the policyholder.
If the policyholder has died, the legal heirs file the claim with a death certificate and proof of inheritance.

## 8.2 Are electronic receipts accepted?
Yes. PDF or photo copies of receipts are accepted for claims up to NT$30,000.
For larger claims, the original paper receipts must be mailed to the claims department.

## 8.3 How are foreign-currency receipts converted?
Amounts in foreign currency are converted to New Taiwan dollars at the Bank of Taiwan selling rate
on the date the expense was incurred, not on the date the claim is filed.

## 8.4 How can a customer check the status of a claim?
Customers can check status in the Northwind app or by calling customer service with their policy number.
Staff should not give a payout estimate before the review is complete.

## 8.5 What happens when a claim is only partly approved?
The decision letter lists each item and the reason it was not paid.
The customer may appeal within 30 days of receiving the letter.
